Privacy Policy

At My Piano by Evostackr, accessible from https://my-piano.evostackr.in/, the privacy of our visitors and musicians is paramount. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is (and is not) collected, how local browser audio synthesis operates, and our strict dedication to user privacy.

🛡️

Summary: My Piano runs 100% client-side in your web browser. We do not require registration, passwords, or emails. We never record your audio performances, and all sound synthesis is processed strictly in your local device memory.

01. No Personal Data Collection

My Piano is engineered as an open, friction-free musical tool. You can access all 88 keys, 7 octaves, keyboard shortcuts, glissando dragging, and chord playing without providing any personal identifiable information (PII).

  • No Accounts or Sign-Ups: We do not require usernames, passwords, phone numbers, or social media logins.
  • No Financial Data: My Piano is 100% free. We never request bank cards, billing addresses, or payment processor info.
  • No Profiles or Tracking IDs: We do not construct behavioral profiles, user histories, or tracking fingerprints across sessions.

02. Local Web Audio API & Sound Processing

All piano acoustics and synthesized sounds are generated entirely on your local computer or mobile device utilizing the standard HTML5 Web Audio API:

  • No Recording: Key presses, melodies, improvisations, and chord progressions are never recorded, captured, or transmitted.
  • No Microphone Access: My Piano does not request or use microphone or webcam permissions.
  • Local Sound Buffers: Compressed audio samples (such as acoustic grand piano tones in OGG format) are fetched statically and held in temporary browser memory (RAM) solely for low-latency playback.

03. Cookies and Local Storage

We prioritize minimal, strictly functional storage:

  • Zero Profiling Cookies: We do not deploy third-party advertising tracking cookies or cross-site tracking beacons.
  • Local Session State: Transient browser storage (such as localStorage or session memory) is utilized solely to preserve user preferences—such as your active keyboard octave, volume level, or whether keyboard shortcut badges are shown.
  • Clearing Storage: You can wipe all local storage data at any time via your browser's standard cache/cookie clearance tools.

04. Web Analytics and Performance Metrics

To maintain sub-15ms audio responsiveness and detect browser compatibility issues, we may collect aggregated, non-identifiable technical metrics (such as browser type, screen viewport, and audio latency). This technical data cannot be connected to any individual person and is used strictly to optimize speed and instrument reliability.

05. Advertising and Third-Party Links

To keep My Piano free and sustainable, non-intrusive advertisements or affiliate recommendations may be presented. These third-party ad networks may utilize standard cookies or web beacons according to their respective privacy disclosures. We encourage you to review their policies or utilize browser ad-preference controls if you prefer personalized opt-outs.

06. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

My Piano is an educational musical instrument widely used in classrooms, homeschooling, and by beginning musicians of all ages:

  • We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 in accordance with the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child protection regulations.
  • Because no personal data is collected from any user, young students can safely explore piano theory without privacy exposure.

07. GDPR & CCPA Compliance (Your Rights)

Under European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), individuals have rights regarding their personal data, including the right to know, access, and delete information.

Because My Piano does not collect or retain personal data, there is no personal database to query or delete. However, if you have any questions regarding privacy standards, you are welcome to reach out to our team at any time.

08. Policy Updates

Evostackr reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features, infrastructure updates, or legal compliance mandates. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
